## A better way to experience your library.

**Jeva** is an independent Windows client for Jellyfin, bringing a clean, streaming-inspired experience to the movies, shows, and videos on your own server.

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/screenshots/home.png" width="100%" alt="Jeva home screen with a cinematic featured title and personalized media rows">
</p>

### Made for the way you watch.

- **A home that feels like yours.** Choose which libraries appear on your home screen, and personalize the sections and their order.
- **A cinematic interface.** Explore featured titles, artwork, movie details, and TV shows in a modern streaming-style layout.
- **Personalize every detail.** Switch between themes, featured layouts, and content display styles.
- **Find what matters.** Browse individual libraries, search your collection, and filter or sort media.
- **More of your media.** Browse music videos and local extras alongside your movies and TV shows.
- **Your language, your region.** Use Jeva in 15 languages, with region-aware age ratings.

## Get started.

1. Download the latest Windows installer from [Releases](https://github.com/tearerry/Jeva/releases).
2. Install and launch Jeva.
3. Connect to your Jellyfin server and enjoy your library.

**Platform:** Windows x64.

## Developer Mode.

Jeva includes a hidden Developer Mode for advanced library management, including refreshing metadata and editing media information.

To enable it, open **Settings → About** and click the **Jeva icon** several times.
