## And the details

- **Subtitles from the first frame**<br>Read straight from the file, and ready wherever you skip.
- **Your language, your ratings**<br>15 languages, with age ratings shown the way your country does.
- **Home, your way**<br>Choose which libraries appear on Home, and give each one its own tab.
- **Spoiler protection**<br>Blur the synopses of episodes you haven't watched, library by library.
- **Extras and photos**<br>Trailers and behind-the-scenes on title pages, and photo albums full screen.
- **Right where you left off**<br>Continue Watching and the next episode, always in sync with your server.

## Get started

You'll need Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and a Jellyfin, Plex or Emby server.

1. Download Jeva from the <a href="https://github.com/tearerry/Jeva/releases/latest">latest release</a>.
2. Open it and choose your server — servers on your network appear automatically.
3. Sign in and start watching.

Prefer not to install? The portable ZIP runs from any folder and keeps its data right beside it.

## Developer Mode

Edit metadata and artwork, or scan your libraries, right from the app. To turn it on, open <strong>Settings → About</strong> and click the Jeva icon seven times in a row.
